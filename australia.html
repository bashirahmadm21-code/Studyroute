Study in Australia

Complete guide to studying in Australia, including universities, tuition fees, scholarships, admission requirements, student visa and application planning.

🇦🇺 Why Study in Australia?

Australia is a major international study destination with universities and other education providers offering undergraduate, postgraduate, research and vocational programs.

Important for international students:

Admission requirements, tuition fees and English requirements depend on the university and course. Always check the exact program before applying.

🎓 Study Levels

Bachelor's, Master's, Doctoral, pathway and vocational programs.

🗣️ Language

Most university courses are taught in English.

💵 Currency

Australian Dollar (AUD)

💰 Tuition Fees in Australia

Australian tuition fees are not one fixed amount. They vary according to the university, course, study level and location.

🎓 Bachelor's

Fees vary significantly by university and subject. Engineering, medicine, business and other disciplines may have different fees.

🎓 Master's

Master's tuition varies by university, course length and field of study.

🔬 PhD

Research students may be eligible for university or government scholarships that can reduce or cover tuition costs.

Don't use a single fee for every university.

Before applying, check the official course page for the exact annual international tuition fee. Course fees can also change between intakes.

🏠 Living Costs in Australia

Actual living expenses depend on your city, accommodation, transport, food and lifestyle. Sydney and Melbourne can have different costs from smaller cities and regional areas.

🏠 Accommodation

Rent is usually one of the largest expenses for international students.

🍎 Food

Food costs depend on whether you cook, eat out or use student meal options.

🚇 Transport

Transport costs depend on the city, distance and available student concessions.

Visa financial requirement:

For a Student visa, the current minimum living-cost figure for the primary student is AUD 29,710 for 12 months. This is a visa financial-capacity figure and should not be treated as a complete estimate of every student's actual living expenses.

🎓 Scholarships in Australia

Scholarships may be available from the Australian Government, universities and other organisations.

🇦🇺 Australia Awards

Australian Government-funded scholarships are available to eligible students from selected developing countries.

🏫 University Scholarships

Many Australian universities offer scholarships or tuition discounts for eligible international students.

🔬 Research Scholarships

Research Master's and PhD applicants may find funding through university research scholarships and the Australian Government Research Training Program.

Scholarship tip:

Search the official university scholarship page for your exact course. Eligibility, deadlines and funding amounts differ between institutions.

📋 Admission Requirements

Requirements vary by university and course. Common documents can include:

  • Valid passport
  • Previous degree, diploma or academic qualification
  • Academic transcripts
  • CV / Resume where required
  • Statement of Purpose or personal statement where required
  • Proof of English proficiency where required
  • Recommendation letters where required
  • Portfolio or additional documents for selected programs
Important:

Meeting the minimum requirements does not automatically guarantee admission. Each university and course can apply its own academic requirements.

🗣️ IELTS, PTE & English Requirements

English requirements depend on both the education provider and the visa requirements.

IELTS

IELTS Academic is commonly accepted by Australian universities. The exact score depends on the course.

PTE Academic

PTE Academic is among the English tests accepted for Australian Student visa English requirements.

Other Tests

The Australian Government accepts several approved English tests for visa purposes, subject to current rules.

MOI / Medium of Instruction:

An MOI letter may be accepted by some Australian universities for admission, but MOI acceptance is not universal. More importantly, university admission requirements and Australian Student visa English requirements are separate matters. Always check both before applying.

🛂 Australia Student Visa — Subclass 500

The Student visa (subclass 500) is the main visa for international students studying in Australia.

📄 CoE

You generally need a Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE) for your intended course when applying for the Student visa.

💰 Financial Capacity

You must be able to demonstrate sufficient funds according to the current visa rules.

🏥 OSHC

Overseas Student Health Cover is required for the duration of your stay, subject to applicable exceptions.

Student visa application charge AUD 2,500*
Student living-cost figure AUD 29,710 / 12 months
Standard work limit 48 hours / fortnight

*The visa charge shown is the amount published from 1 July 2026. Fees and requirements can change.

Do not depend on part-time work for your visa funding.

The Department of Home Affairs states that students should not rely on work to support themselves or their family. You should have appropriate financial capacity for your stay.

Official Student Visa Information

💼 Work While Studying

Student visa holders can generally work while studying, subject to visa conditions.

Current standard limit:

Most Student visa holders can work up to 48 hours per fortnight while their course is in session. Different rules apply during scheduled course breaks, and Master's by Research and Doctoral students have different work-hour arrangements after their course has started.

Always check your individual visa conditions through the official Department of Home Affairs information.

🎓 After Graduation — Temporary Graduate Visa

Eligible international graduates may be able to apply for a Temporary Graduate visa (subclass 485) after completing an eligible Australian qualification.

🎓 Post-Higher Education Work

This stream is for eligible graduates with a degree-level qualification or higher.

🔧 Post-Vocational Education Work

This stream applies to eligible graduates with certain associate degree, diploma or trade qualifications.

🌏 Regional Options

Eligible graduates may have access to a second Post-Higher Education Work stream where the relevant regional requirements are met.

Important:

Post-study visa rules can change. Graduation does not automatically guarantee a Temporary Graduate visa. Check the current eligibility requirements before planning around it.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can Pakistani students study in Australia?

Yes. Pakistani students can apply to eligible Australian education providers and, after meeting the relevant requirements, apply for the Student visa subclass 500.

Is IELTS mandatory for every Australian university?

Not necessarily. Universities set their own admission requirements and may accept different forms of English evidence. However, the Australian Government has separate English requirements for the Student visa. Check both before applying.

Can Australia accept MOI?

Some universities may consider an MOI letter or other previous-study evidence, but this is institution- and course-specific. MOI should not automatically be treated as a replacement for every English requirement.

How much money is required for an Australian Student visa?

The current published 12-month living-cost figure for the primary student is AUD 29,710. You also need to consider course fees, travel and other applicable costs.

How many hours can international students work?

The standard Student visa work limit is 48 hours per fortnight while the course is in session. Different rules can apply to research Master's and Doctoral students.

How much is the Australian Student visa fee?

From 1 July 2026, the standard Student visa application charge is AUD 2,500, subject to applicable exemptions and concessions.

Do I need a CoE for the Student visa?

Yes. A Confirmation of Enrolment is generally required for the intended course when lodging a Student visa application.

Can I stay in Australia after graduation?

Eligible graduates may be able to apply for a Temporary Graduate visa subclass 485. Eligibility, stream and stay period depend on the current rules and the qualification completed.
