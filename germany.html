🇩🇪 Study in Germany for International Students

Germany is a major study destination for international students, with universities offering programs in engineering, technology, sciences, business, humanities and many other fields.

Programs may be taught in German or English. Always check the exact language and admission requirements of your chosen program before applying.

🎓 Study Levels

Bachelor's, Master's and doctoral programs are available across German higher-education institutions.

🗣️ Language

German and English-taught programs are available. Requirements depend on the specific course.

💶 Currency

Germany uses the Euro (€).

Exact requirements depend on your university, degree program and educational background.

🛂 Passport

🗣️ Language Proof

📝 Motivation Letter

📑 CV

📚 Additional Documents

Certain programs may require portfolios, recommendation letters, entrance tests or other documents.

🗣️ Can You Apply with MOI?

MOI (Medium of Instruction) acceptance is not universal across Germany. Some universities or programs may accept alternative evidence of previous English-medium education, while others require an accepted language certificate.

⚠️ Important: Do not assume that MOI is accepted everywhere. Check the exact language requirement of your chosen program on the official university website before applying.

🌐 What is uni-assist?

uni-assist processes applications for many German universities from applicants with international educational certificates. However, not every German university or every program uses uni-assist.

📌 Remember: The university decides whether your application should be submitted through uni-assist. Always check the university's official application instructions.

🛂 Germany Student Visa

Students from countries that require a visa should follow the current requirements of the competent German mission.

💳 Financial Proof: The German Federal Foreign Office states that study visa applicants can demonstrate financing through accepted methods such as parents' financial circumstances, a declaration of commitment, a blocked account or qualifying scholarship funding.

Visa documentation and procedures can change. Check the German mission responsible for your place of residence before submitting your application.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions About Studying in Germany

Is Germany free for international students?

Many public universities do not charge tuition for many Bachelor's and Master's programs, but exceptions exist. Students should check the exact university, program and federal-state rules.

How much does it cost to study in Germany?

Costs include living expenses, accommodation, semester contributions and, where applicable, tuition fees. The total depends on the city, university and student's lifestyle.

Can I study in Germany with MOI?

It depends on the exact university and program. Some may accept alternative English-language evidence, while others require a specific language certificate.

Do all German universities use uni-assist?

No. Some universities use uni-assist while others have their own application systems.

Are scholarships available in Germany?

Yes, scholarship and funding opportunities exist, including opportunities listed through DAAD and other organizations. Eligibility varies.

Do I need a student visa for Germany?

Visa requirements depend on nationality, residence and the purpose and duration of your stay. Check the current requirements of the responsible German mission.

What documents are needed for German university admission?

Requirements vary, but may include academic records, transcripts, language evidence, passport and additional documents specified by the university or program.
