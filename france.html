Study in France

Complete guide to studying in France, including universities, tuition fees, living costs, scholarships, admission requirements and student visa planning.

🇫🇷 Why Study in France?

France offers a wide range of higher education opportunities through public universities, grandes écoles and other higher education institutions. Students can find programs in many fields, including engineering, technology, business, science, humanities and medicine.

For students from Pakistan:

Pakistan is covered by the French Études en France (EeF) procedure for most applicants. Always check the current Campus France Pakistan rules for your specific study level and program.

🎓 Study Levels

Bachelor's, Master's and PhD programs are available.

🗣️ Language

French and English-taught programs are available, depending on the institution and program.

💶 Currency

Euro (€)

💶 Tuition Fees in France

Tuition fees vary according to the institution, program and student's status. For the 2026/2027 academic year, Campus France lists the following differentiated public tuition fees for many non-EU students.

Bachelor / Licence €2,902 / year
Master €3,950 / year
PhD / Doctorate €398 / year
Important:

These figures apply to the relevant public institutions and student categories described by Campus France. Some international students may be exempt from differentiated fees, while private institutions can have substantially different tuition fees.

Always verify the exact tuition fee directly with your chosen institution before applying.

🏠 Living Costs in France

Living costs depend heavily on the city and type of accommodation. Paris and other large cities can be more expensive than smaller cities.
