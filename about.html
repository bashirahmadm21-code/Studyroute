🎓 About StudyRoute

StudyRoute is a simple study-abroad guidance platform designed to help students explore international education opportunities.

🌍 What We Do

We provide useful information about studying abroad, including study destinations, estimated costs, scholarships, application guidance, and helpful resources for students.

📚 Our Goal

Our goal is to make the study-abroad planning process easier to understand by bringing useful information and tools together in one place.

💬 Student Guidance

Students can submit an inquiry through our guidance form. We use the information they provide to understand their questions and respond with relevant guidance.
