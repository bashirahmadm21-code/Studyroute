Study Abroad Roadmap

Follow these basic steps when planning your study abroad journey.

Step 1 — Choose Your Country

Compare tuition fees, living costs, universities and visa requirements.

Step 2 — Find Universities

Check programs that match your academic qualification and field.

Step 3 — Prepare Documents

Prepare your degree, transcript, passport, CV, motivation letter and language proof where required.

Step 4 — Apply

Submit your application before the university deadline and keep your application documents organized.

Step 5 — Scholarship & Visa

Check scholarship opportunities and prepare your student visa documents.
