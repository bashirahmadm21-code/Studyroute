🇭🇺 Why Study in Hungary?

Hungary is a popular European destination for international students, offering bachelor's, master's and doctoral programs across a wide range of academic fields. Many Hungarian universities also offer programs taught in English.

🎓 Study Levels

Bachelor's, Master's and PhD programs.

🗣️ Language

Hungarian and English programs are available.

💶 Currency

Hungarian Forint (HUF)

🎓 Popular Universities in Hungary

Examples of well-known Hungarian universities.

Eötvös Loránd University

One of Hungary's major universities, offering programs across sciences, humanities and social sciences.

University of Debrecen

Offers a wide range of programs for international students across multiple academic fields.

University of Szeged

A major Hungarian university with programs in sciences, medicine, humanities and other fields.

Budapest University of Technology and Economics

Known for engineering, technology, architecture and scientific programs.

Semmelweis University

A well-known institution focused on medicine, health sciences and related fields.

Corvinus University of Budapest

Offers programs in business, economics, management and related areas.

🎓 Scholarships & Financial Support

🏛️ University Scholarships

🎓 Stipendium Hungaricum

Hungary's Stipendium Hungaricum program provides scholarship opportunities for eligible international students.

💶 Other Funding

Students can research grants, scholarships and other eligible funding opportunities.

📋 Common Application Requirements

Exact requirements depend on the university and program.

📄 Academic Documents

Previous qualifications, transcripts and academic records may be required.

🛂 Passport

A valid passport is normally required for international applications.

🗣️ Language Proof

Programs may require proof of Hungarian or English language ability.

📝 Motivation Letter

Some programs require a motivation letter or statement of purpose.

📑 CV

A CV may be required, especially for postgraduate applications.

💳 Financial Documents

Financial evidence may be required during the visa or residence permit process.
